What we offer

Care designed around you

From acute pain relief to long-term wellness, we meet you where you are.

01

Targeted Chiropractic Treatment

This targeted session is designed to get keep you moving and often used for routine care. This appointment includes focused spinal manipulation and often a single service muscle work (like Cupping, FDM, ART, trigger point work) on one specific area, or expert Kinesio Taping application.

02

Advanced Functional Therapy

Our signature care session leveraging advanced functional techniques to accelerate healing and eliminate pain. This time block allows for a customized blend of a standard chiropractic adjustment with either Class 4 Laser Therapy, Graston, Fascial Distortion Model (FDM), or localized Active Release Techniques (ART).

03

Elite Injury Evaluation & Master Treatment

Ideal for a new injury evaluation, a major re-examination, or a multi-region breakdown. This long-form premium session gives your provider time to run full functional movement screenings, followed by a masterful combination of shockwave, laser, or needling, alongside heavy manual therapy and specific joint manipulation.

04

Premium Multimodal Therapy

An immersive, high-yield treatment tailored for complex sports injuries, chronic pain, and full-body movement restoration. This comprehensive block is required for intensive Dry Needling sessions or Shockwave Therapy, often paired with deep ART soft tissue release, therapeutic stretching, or cupping.
